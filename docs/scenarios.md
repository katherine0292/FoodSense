# Dinner decision scenarios

**FoodSense · Shangcheng (Katherine) Lin**

Maya is an illustrative scenario character. [Wei Lin](research/persona.md) is the composite persona used to frame the product.

## Current routine

On the train home after work, Maya thinks about making chicken and rice. She cannot see what is still in the fridge and is unsure when she bought the chicken. At home she finds she cannot use it. Eggs and other groceries are still available, but she is tired and has no plan for using them. She orders takeaway and leaves the remaining ingredients for another day.

## Proposed routine

Before reaching home, Maya checks the last available shelf view and the Use first list. The app shows the chicken's recorded date separately from any possible device cue. Maya excludes the chicken and checks what else is available. FoodSense surfaces egg fried rice in the early walkthrough using the sample pantry. The details show the required ingredients and make gaps visible before she starts. She follows the steps, runs the timer, and confirms which ingredients she fully used.

| Beat | Maya's action | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Commute | Thinks about dinner and opens the app. | Today shows food to use first. |
| 2. Check | Looks for the chicken and verifies what she can use. | The shelf and date information are available separately. |
| 3. Choose | Reviews a quick meal she can make with her eggs and other ingredients. | The match shows time and what is available or missing. |
| 4. Cook | Opens the recipe and works through the steps. | The timer and step controls stay accessible. |
| 5. Update | Confirms what she used up. | The pantry reflects the items she removes. |
