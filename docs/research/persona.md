# Wei Lin

**Composite persona · FoodSense**

Wei is a young professional living in a shared house in Ultimo, Sydney. She cooks four to five times a week, buys fresh produce and familiar staples at a local Asian grocer, and works with limited storage and a fixed budget. She often returns to the same few dishes because searching for something new takes time and a recipe can call for ingredients she does not have.

| Moment | Need |
| --- | --- |
| After shopping | Put new groceries away quickly and confirm what is recorded. |
| Before dinner | Know what is available and what to use first without emptying the fridge. |
| While deciding | Find an achievable recipe using partial ingredients and ordinary tools. |
| After cooking | Keep an accurate pantry without logging every small change. |

The design implications are low-effort intake, a visible use-first list, portion control and recipe suggestions grounded in the pantry. The persona represents a design scenario, not an individual participant or a measured profile.
