# Interview record B

**Frequent solo cooking, limited storage and interrupted routines**

The participant works in media, moved to Sydney within the previous year, and shares an apartment. The responses below are anonymised English paraphrases of the 15 interview prompts.

| Topic | Response summary |
| --- | --- |
| Household and eating habits | Enjoys trying recipes at home. Fresh greens feel expensive, so they aim to buy only what they can use. |
| Cooking frequency | Cooks four to five days a week and favours simple home meals over expensive takeaway. |
| Meal decisions | Checks what needs using or what is on sale. Sometimes changes plans after seeing a recipe online. |
| Weekly pattern | Does a main weekend shop and portions food. Work deadlines can derail planned meals, leaving ingredients untouched. |
| Shopping | Keeps a phone list, but discounted packs are hard to pass up and can exceed available space or portions. |
| Inventory | Looks by eye and keeps commonly used items at the front. Food and bottles at the back are hard to see. |
| Date checks | Usually only checks thoroughly after an odour or visible spoilage, around once a month. |
| Forgotten ingredients | Potatoes and onions tucked away are easy to forget until they sprout or spoil. |
| Local shopping | Prices, large packages and trips for specialty seasonings make it harder to cook familiar food on a budget. |
| Effect of sharing | Items get moved by housemates. Limited shelf and counter space create friction. |
| Waste and budget | Connects even small food losses to a meaningful cost over time. |
| Stressful moments | Arriving hungry to find an unusable vegetable or missing seasoning can end in takeaway. |
| Existing tools | Stopped using inventory apps after a few days because manual date entry was tedious. Phone notes are easier but provide no freshness reminders. |
| Desired help | Would like recognition or sensor assistance, a remote shelf view, and simple recipes using ingredients that need attention. |
| Desired change | Wants less inventory upkeep and a routine that makes cooking at home easier under pressure. |
