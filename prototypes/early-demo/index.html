Egg Fried Rice
⏱ 15 min · 🔥 320 kcal · 2 servings
⚠️ 3 expiring items
Quick
Ingredients
3 Eggs
2 cups cooked Rice
1 handful Spinach
2 tbsp Soy Sauce
1 tbsp Sesame Oil
Spring onions to garnish
Steps
1
Heat sesame oil in a wok over high heat until smoking.
2
Scramble eggs in the wok, pushing to the side once half-cooked.
3
Add cold rice, breaking up any clumps. Stir-fry 2 minutes.
4
Add spinach and soy sauce. Toss everything until spinach wilts.

Pad Thai
⏱ 25 min · 🔥 480 kcal · 2 servings
⚠️ 4 expiring items
High Protein
Ingredients
200g Rice Noodles
2 Eggs
100g Mushrooms, sliced
1 handful Spinach
3 tbsp Pad Thai sauce
Steps
1
Soak noodles in warm water for 10 minutes. Drain.
2
Stir-fry mushrooms in oil over high heat for 2 minutes.
3
Push to side, scramble eggs, then mix with mushrooms.
4
Add noodles and sauce. Toss well. Add spinach last.
